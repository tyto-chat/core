## 1. Data controller

The Operator of %server_name% determines how and why your personal data is
processed. Contact for privacy matters: %contact_email%.

## 2. What we collect

- **Account data** — the email address and display name you provide, a hashed
  password, and account timestamps.
- **Content** — the messages, replies, reactions, and files you send, and the
  communities and channels you join.
- **Technical data** — data needed to operate the service, such as
  authentication tokens, session and device information for notifications, and
  limited logs used for security and abuse prevention.
- **Age confirmation** — if the Operator has enabled a minimum-age gate, your
  date of birth is checked at registration but is **not stored**; only the fact
  that the check passed is recorded.

## 3. Why we process it (legal bases)

- To **provide the service** you signed up for (performance of a contract).
- To keep the service **secure** and prevent abuse (legitimate interests).
- To meet **legal obligations**, including handling reports and lawful
  requests.
- With your **consent**, where required — for example optional notifications.

## 4. How it is used

Your data is used to operate the service: to authenticate you, deliver your
messages, show you communities and notifications, enforce the Terms of
Service, and keep the service safe. Your data is **not sold**.

## 5. Sharing

Content you post is visible to the audiences you send it to (community members,
channel members, or direct-message recipients). The Operator relies on
infrastructure providers (such as hosting and, if configured, push,
voice, and email providers) that process data on the Operator's behalf. The
Operator may disclose data where required by law.

## 6. Retention

Data is kept for as long as your account exists or as needed to operate the
service. The Operator may configure automatic retention limits after which
older message content and notifications are removed. When you delete your
account, your personal data is deleted or anonymized, subject to a short grace
period and any records the Operator must keep by law.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export,
delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data, and to object to
certain processing. The service provides tools to export and delete your
account; for other requests, contact %contact_email%. You also have the right
to complain to your local data-protection authority.

## 8. International transfers

Where your data is processed outside your country, the Operator is responsible
for ensuring an appropriate legal basis and safeguards for that transfer.

## 9. Security

The Operator takes reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect
your data. No system is perfectly secure; report any suspected vulnerability to
the security contact listed in the project's security policy.

## 10. Children

The service is not directed to children below the minimum age set by the
Operator. If you believe a child has provided personal data without
appropriate consent, contact %contact_email%.

## 11. Changes

This Policy may be updated. Material changes will be communicated through the
service where practical. The "last updated" date above reflects the current
